Rules To The Hunt

Knowing the valuable techniques and tricks while you ghost hunt is crucial when trying to get incredible investigation results. Here you'll be able to learn more about what you can do and remember for your ghost hunts.

  • Investigate In Numbers: Investigating in groups or pairs is safer than going solo. Always have a buddy with you when investigating, and be aware of your surroundings.
  • Document Everything: Remember to use all your tools while investigating. This increases the chance of finding actual evidence and could help debunk possible interactions that can be explained.
  • Respect the Location: When looking for a location, be sure to avoid trespassing, as it is not worth getting in trouble with the law. Also, respect your space and try not to be destructive or disrespectful in any way. Always follow local regulations and guidelines, and be safe while investigating.
  • Ask Clear, Respectful Questions: When communicating with spirits, be sure to state simple and straightforward questions for spirits to answer. After you ask a question, give a few moments of silence for a possible response.
  • Use Fresh Batteries: Paranormal spirits and entities are known to drain equipment, so be vigilant and ensure your devices are fully charged before starting. Packing extra batteries as well is recommended.
  • Remain Objective: Be sure not to make quick assumptions that every noise or EMF spike is paranormal activity. Check your space and rule out any possible natural causes first, then make your assumptions.
  • Use Trigger Objects: Many investigators will bring items like toys, coins, or personal objects that can help spike or provoke paranormal interactions. This is especially helpful if you know a location's history and understand what objects to bring.
  • Stay Grounded and Calm: Don’t let fear or adrenaline cloud your mind. Be calm and stay focused on gathering evidence. However, on the off-chance that you feel unsafe and the activity you are receiving is negative, calmly leave the space and/or go outside of the location to release any tension.
  • Know Your History: Knowing a location's history can be extremely important when investigating. It will provide you with knowledge of the location that can be used when asking questions, calling out certain spirits, and even understanding any misfortunes of the location. It is recommended to do your own research on the location, or if one is offered, to go on a ghost tour and expand your knowledge further.
  • When Diving into the Demonic: You may have heard of malevolent spirits in the ghost-hunting world. If you happen to come across something that seems negative and begin to feel sick, it is a rule to pause your investigation and leave the space immediately. If possible, step outside and take a breather to regain your composure. It can be expected that intense energies held in a location, especially those filled with tragedy, will feel overwhelming and cause nausea, so remain calm and remove yourself accordingly from the space.
  • Stay Away From Inexperienced Seances and Cursed Items: It may seem exciting or intriguing at first. However, it is best to remain clear of items that aren't necessarily safe and/or practices you know nothing about. Cursed items, such as dibbuk boxes, cursed dolls, remedies, and other unsafe items, shouldn't be brought with you while hunting and can even intentionally harm you if used incorrectly. It is safer to have a professional experienced in the history and handling of such items to be present. Inexperienced users should not conduct seances. A medium or professional in the field should be the one to lead and conduct a seance if wishing to perform one.

By following these rules, tips, and tricks, you'll perfect your skills in ghost-hunting, promoting incredible experiences that increase your chances of gathering evidence while staying safe and focused.
