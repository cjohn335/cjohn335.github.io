A Guide to Ghost Hunting and Paranormal Investigations

Have you ever wanted to expand your knowledge of the paranormal? Or get out in the field and investigate for yourself? Then here, you’ll find everything you’re looking for to finally get started. Whether you are a well-rounded professional or a beginner learning the ropes, here you will be provided with valuable information and history for your future ghost-hunting endeavors.

Getting Started

When getting started, ghost hunting requires basic equipment and preparation for a long night of exploration. To begin planning, you'll want to choose a recognized haunted location, preferably one close by. If you are curious about where to start, you can check out some of these locations here. After deciding on the date and location, you must gather the essential tools like a flashlight, an EMF meter, a digital recorder, and a camera.

Remember to bring spare memory cards, batteries, or any other electrical components for your gear! A common assumption when investigating is the idea that things will instantly start to happen. This is not the case, as patience is key. Sometimes, investigators can go to locations and receive nothing too significant, while other locations will provide the most memorable nights to last a lifetime.

Ghost hunting can offer an exciting blend of discovery, thrill, and mystery!

Even if you are trying to find a spooky activity with your friends for the fall season, ghost hunting is something everyone should experience once in their life — you might even gather the incredible evidence you've been looking for. Whether you're a beginner or a pro, knowing the proper approach and tools will give you the skills to ghost hunt confidently. Happy Hunting!
